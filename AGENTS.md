# Standing rule

External links in posts must use HTML `<a>` tags with `target="_blank"` and `rel="noopener"`. Preserve existing `rel` values by adding `noopener` where needed. Leave internal links unchanged. Do not rewrite existing posts unless requested.

## Blog prose

Preserve edited prose outside the requested changes. The author's latest instructions take precedence over earlier drafts or briefs.

## Post URLs

For new posts, use the full title word for word as the URL slug, lowercased with hyphens between words. Do not shorten it to a working title or topic label. Leave published URLs unchanged unless the author requests a migration.
